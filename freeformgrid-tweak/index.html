FreeformGrid 1.0.3: Home Screen Icon Placement on Rootless iOS 17 Jailbreaks

FreeformGrid 1.0.3 is an updated Home Screen tweak by emp0ry. Its package description says it places Home Screen icons in any open grid position on rootless iOS 17 jailbreaks. The listed architecture is iphoneos-arm64, and the dependency field lists MobileSubstrate, PreferenceLoader, firmware greater than or equal to 17.0, and firmware less than 18.0.
