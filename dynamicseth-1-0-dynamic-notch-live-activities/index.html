DynamicSeth 1.0 Brings a Dynamic Island-Style Notch to iOS 15–17

Whoops’ DynamicSeth builds customizable Now Playing controls, calls, system notices and shortcuts around a physical or virtual notch. The first public Havoc release targets conventional rootless jailbreaks and includes a device-information disclosure for purchase verification.

By Zeeshan BarviPublished October 27, 2026Verified against the official Havoc page and package index
Latest verified release: Havoc lists DynamicSeth 1.0, published October 25, 2026, for $1.99. The listing declares iOS 15.0–17.7.11, rootless support and a jailbreak requirement. Havoc’s package index provides one iphoneos-arm64 build.

What DynamicSeth 1.0 does

DynamicSeth turns the area around the screen cutout into an expandable activity surface. Its official feature list includes a virtual notch for devices without one, notch appearance and motion controls, Now Playing, calls, Airplane Mode and other notices, plus gestures such as a notch peek and a hold action for the flashlight.

This is not Apple’s native Dynamic Island and it does not change the physical display. It is a jailbreak tweak drawing its own interface around the existing notch—or around a software-created virtual notch. The result and available space will depend on the device’s screen shape and the chosen layout.

What changed before the public 1.0 release

DynamicSeth’s only public version is 1.0. Its official changelog documents changes made after an earlier build was rejected during Havoc review:

  • The demo tour now works without an active license.
  • The main settings page now shows whether the license is active.
  • The activation retry control was moved from About to the main page and renamed Confirm Purchase.
  • The Device Unregistered alert now remains visible until dismissed.
  • Text truncation and several other bugs were addressed.

Those notes describe pre-release review changes inside the first public version; they are not evidence of an older public 0.x package.

Compatibility is narrower than “iOS 15–17”

The live Havoc page lists iOS 15.0 through 17.7.11, rootless support and a jailbreak requirement. The package index adds the concrete architecture and dependencies: iphoneos-arm64, firmware 15.0 or later but below 18.0, MobileSubstrate or ElleKit, and PreferenceLoader.

That identifies a conventional rootless jailbreak package. It does not prove RootHide compatibility: Havoc does not expose a separate iphoneos-arm64e build for DynamicSeth. Do not convert or inject the paid package into another environment unless the developer explicitly supports that workflow.

The developer reports testing on an iPhone 7 with iOS 15.8.5, iPhone X with iOS 16.7.16 and iPhone 11 with iOS 17.0. These are the developer’s test devices—not a complete compatibility matrix. The listed firmware range also does not mean every iPhone on iOS 15–17 has a public jailbreak.

Privacy and license verification

The developer explicitly states that DynamicSeth sends the device’s UDID and model to the developer’s license server, which checks the purchase with Havoc. The developer also says that nothing is stored. This article can verify that the disclosure appears on the official product page; it cannot independently audit the server or its retention behavior.

A UDID uniquely identifies a device. Buyers who are not comfortable sending it to a separate developer-operated licensing service should not install the tweak. Havoc purchase delivery and DynamicSeth’s additional license check are separate parts of the transaction.

Installation checklist

  1. Confirm that the exact iPhone and firmware have a compatible conventional rootless jailbreak.
  2. Refresh the official Havoc repository in Sileo or Zebra.
  3. Open DynamicSeth and verify version 1.0, package ID com.whoops.dynamicseth, the $1.99 price and iphoneos-arm64 architecture.
  4. Read the UDID and model disclosure before completing the purchase.
  5. Install only from Havoc, respring, then begin with the demo tour and default layout.
  6. Enable one notice or gesture at a time. If the status area overlaps or SpringBoard becomes unstable, disable DynamicSeth before combining it with another notch or status-bar tweak.

For package-manager steps, use the Sileo tweak installation guide. Before buying, run through the tweak compatibility checklist. Users comparing a smaller status-bar treatment can also review LilyWhite’s status capsule.

Who is DynamicSeth for?

DynamicSeth is most relevant to notched iPhones that lack Apple’s native Dynamic Island, especially when the user wants Now Playing controls, system notices and quick actions collected around the cutout. The virtual-notch option also lets devices without a notch use the interface, although it consumes status-bar space that was not physically reserved for a cutout.

It is less compelling on a device that already has a native Dynamic Island, uses another notch or status-bar tweak, runs RootHide without an official package, or cannot use a supported jailbreak. Because 1.0 is the first public release, long-term stability, battery impact and compatibility with every notification or media app are not yet established by the official changelog.

Frequently asked questions

Does DynamicSeth replace the physical notch?

No. It draws a customizable activity interface around the existing cutout or creates a software-only virtual notch.

Does DynamicSeth support iOS 18 or iOS 26?

No. Havoc currently lists iOS 15.0 through 17.7.11, and the package dependency excludes firmware 18.0 or later.

Is DynamicSeth available for RootHide?

Not through a separately documented package. The official index exposes iphoneos-arm64, not a RootHide iphoneos-arm64e build.

What device information does the license check send?

The developer says it sends the device UDID and model to the developer’s license server to check the purchase with Havoc, and says that nothing is stored.

Was DynamicSeth tested on every compatible iPhone?

No. The developer names three test devices: iPhone 7 on iOS 15.8.5, iPhone X on iOS 16.7.16 and iPhone 11 on iOS 17.0.
