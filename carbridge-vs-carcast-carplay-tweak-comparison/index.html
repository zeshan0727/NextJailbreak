CarBridge vs CarCast: Which CarPlay Tweak Fits Your Jailbreak?

CarBridge and CarCast both put non-CarPlay iPhone apps on a vehicle display, but their official compatibility ranges barely overlap. Your firmware—and CarCast’s required Airaw dependency—decides the practical winner before feature preference does.

By Zeeshan Barvi · Published October 16, 2026 · Verified against the official Havoc, Chariz and developer pages

Safety: configure apps while parked. Neither package makes a phone app safe to operate while driving, and local traffic laws still apply. DRM-protected video may show a black screen; this comparison does not claim either tweak bypasses DRM.

The short answer

Use CarBridge on a compatible iOS 13–16 jailbreak. Havoc lists CarBridge 2.0.15 for iOS 13.0 through 16.7.16, marks rootless support, and sells it for $9.99.

Use CarCast on a compatible jailbroken device running iOS 17, iOS 18 or iOS 26. Chariz explicitly says CarCast 1.2 does not support iOS 16 or earlier. It costs $4.99 but requires Airaw, which Chariz lists at $9.99; a new buyer therefore needs both products, totaling $14.98 before tax.

Do not install both. The CarCast page and Dcsyhi’s official repository explicitly warn that CarCast and CarBridge conflict and should not be installed together.

What CarBridge offers

CarBridge’s official promise is direct: “Open any app in CarPlay.” Its Havoc screenshots show ordinary iPhone apps added to the CarPlay app grid and displayed inside the vehicle interface. Version 2.0.15 remains the current Havoc listing, with rootless support marked as available.

The main advantage is simplicity for supported older firmware: one $9.99 product rather than a required platform-plus-add-on pair. The main boundary is equally clear—Havoc stops its compatibility line at iOS 16.7.16. Do not buy it for iOS 17, 18, 26 or 27 based on a third-party mirror claiming wider support.

What CarCast offers

CarCast is a newer $4.99 add-on in Dcsyhi’s Airaw ecosystem. Its settings page lets you add installed apps, remove them, reorder them, apply changes while connected and restart CarPlay when needed. The current 1.2 changelog says app pinning was improved on iOS 17 and iOS 18 or later.

Airaw is not optional for this workflow. CarCast places apps on the CarPlay Home screen; Airaw handles launching them. Airaw 1.7.1 also provides a wider CarPlay customization platform—layout, wallpaper, status-bar controls, widgets and hosted-scene options—but those are Airaw capabilities, not features CarCast alone should be credited with.

Compatibility matters more than the iOS number

A package page supporting a firmware does not prove every device on that firmware has a public jailbreak. CarCast’s official tested list names Dopamine and NathanLR on iOS 17, Dopamine on iOS 18 and iOS 26, plus a rootless environment. Those are developer-reported test environments, not a promise that all processors and point releases are jailbreakable.

Before buying either tweak, check the exact iPhone model, firmware build, active jailbreak, rootless/rootful environment and package architecture. If the device is not jailbroken, neither CarBridge nor CarCast becomes usable merely through TrollStore or ordinary sideloading.

App behavior and DRM limits

Phone apps were not designed for a CarPlay display. CarCast’s official page warns about incorrect layouts, different touch behavior, crashes and failures to launch. It also states that FairPlay-protected Netflix, Prime Video and Disney+ content cannot be displayed and may appear as a black screen. YouTube and Safari can work for ordinary browsing or playback, but protected content can still fail.

CarBridge’s Havoc page does not make a current DRM-bypass claim. Treat the same practical limitation cautiously: adding an app to CarPlay does not remove the app’s own playback protection or guarantee that every control maps correctly.

Before switching between them

  1. Remove the currently installed CarBridge or CarCast package through Sileo before installing the other.
  2. If leaving CarCast, review whether you still use Airaw for layout, widgets or other CarPlay customization before removing it.
  3. Review Sileo’s removal queue and cancel if unrelated core packages are proposed. See the safe Sileo uninstall guide.
  4. Reconnect or restart CarPlay only as the package instructions request, and test while parked.
