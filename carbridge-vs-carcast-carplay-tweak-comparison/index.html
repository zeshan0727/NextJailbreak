CarBridge 2.1.0 vs CarCast 1.2: iOS 18 Support Changes the CarPlay Choice

Havoc announced that CarBridge 2.1.0 adds iOS 18 support and improves stability when switching apps. CarCast 1.2 already lists iOS 17, 18 and 26, but requires Airaw and cannot be installed alongside CarBridge. The exact CarBridge 2.1.0 point-build matrix still needs confirmation.

By Zeeshan Barvi · Published October 16, 2026 · Updated November 24, 2026 · Checked against the official Havoc account, CarBridge FAQ and Chariz product pages

Safety: configure and test CarPlay tweaks while parked. Neither package makes an iPhone app safe to operate while driving. CarCast also documents that FairPlay-protected video can appear black; it does not bypass DRM.

The short answer

CarBridge 2.1.0 is the new iOS 18 announcement. On November 24, the official Havoc Marketplace account said the release adds iOS 18 support and improves stability when switching apps. The post does not publish an exact iOS 18 point-build range or restate whether the new build is rootless, rootful or RootHide-compatible.

CarCast 1.2 has the clearer published compatibility matrix. Chariz says it works on iOS 17, iOS 18 and iOS 26, does not support iOS 16 or earlier, requires a jailbreak and requires Airaw to launch added apps. Chariz also explicitly warns not to install CarCast and CarBridge together.

For an iOS 18 device, both now have an iOS 18 support claim—but the evidence is not equally detailed. CarCast's page lists tested jailbreak environments; CarBridge's new announcement does not yet give a complete build or jailbreak-environment matrix. Check the live package depiction in Sileo/Havoc before buying or upgrading.

CarBridge 2.1.0: what is confirmed

  • Version: 2.1.0, announced by Havoc Marketplace on November 24, 2026.
  • Change 1: added iOS 18 support.
  • Change 2: improved stability when switching apps.
  • Installation: CarBridge's official FAQ says a jailbreak is required and Havoc is the only official store.

There is an important documentation gap. The searchable Havoc package result still exposes older 2.0.x metadata, including a 2.0.15 listing with an iOS 13.0–16.7.16 range. Havoc's older November 1 announcement for 2.0.16 explicitly described iOS 17 support as rootless. The new 2.1.0 post does not give a point-build range or repeat the jailbreak environment. Treat the 2.1.0 announcement as the latest release news, but do not extend it to every iOS 18.x build, iOS 26, iOS 27 or RootHide without an updated package specification.

The Havoc package and version-history HTML endpoints returned HTTP 403 during this check, so their embedded page data and __NEXT_DATA__ fields could not be inspected. This is a retrieval limitation—not evidence that the 2.1.0 release does not exist.

CarCast 1.2: the more explicit iOS 17/18/26 route

CarCast adds installed iPhone apps to the CarPlay Home screen. Its Settings page lets you add, remove and reorder apps; changes can be applied while connected to CarPlay. Airaw handles launching the added apps, so Airaw is a required dependency rather than an optional extra for the CarCast workflow.

Chariz lists these tested environments:

  • iOS 17 with Dopamine and NathanLR;
  • iOS 18 with Dopamine;
  • iOS 26 with Dopamine;
  • a rootless jailbreak environment.

Chariz says CarCast is not compatible with iOS 16 or earlier. Its page lists version 1.2 and says that release improved app-pinning behavior on iOS 17 and iOS 18 or later. The published compatibility statement names iOS 17, 18 and 26; it does not establish iOS 27 support.

Where Airaw fits

Airaw is a separate CarPlay customization platform. Its current Chariz page lists version 1.7.1 and iOS 14–26 support, with rootful and rootless environments and dependencies including ElleKit or Substrate, PreferenceLoader, Cephei and CCSupport.

CarCast needs Airaw to launch apps it adds. CarBridge does not list Airaw as a prerequisite in its official FAQ, although Airaw documents a CarBridge full-screen mode when CarBridge is installed. Do not transfer Airaw's rootful support to CarCast: CarCast's own tested-environment list specifies rootless.

Common CarPlay problems and safe checks

  • CarCast app is listed but will not launch: remove it from CarCast, add it again, then restart SpringBoard as Chariz documents.
  • Added app does not appear: restart CarPlay from CarCast Settings or reconnect the phone and display.
  • Layout or touch behavior is wrong: apps designed for iPhone may not behave like native CarPlay apps. Test while parked and remove apps that are unusable on the display.
  • Protected video is black: CarCast explicitly says FairPlay-protected Netflix, Prime Video and Disney+ video cannot be displayed; it does not bypass DRM.
  • Switching from one bridge to the other: uninstall the current bridge first. Review Sileo's removal queue and do not remove unrelated jailbreak dependencies. See the safe Sileo uninstall guide.

Before installing either package, check the exact iPhone model, iOS build, jailbreak tool and package environment.
