Amethyst v1.1 Fixes App Crashes, A8X Support and A9/A10 Reliability

Amethyst v1.1 is a reliability-focused update for the semi-untethered iOS 12 and iOS 13 jailbreak. It targets a delayed app-crash problem, legacy-device support, exploit consistency and cases where tweaks or fakesigned app frameworks would not load.

By Zeeshan Barvi · Published October 13, 2026 · Verified against the official v1.1 release, repository and installation guide

Compatibility warning: Amethyst is for all devices on iOS 12.0–13.7. A package or tweak supporting those iOS versions does not make Amethyst available on newer firmware. Confirm the exact device and iOS version before installing.

What changed in Amethyst v1.1

The official v1.1 release was published on October 12, 2026. Its most visible fix addresses apps that could begin crashing at launch several hours after jailbreaking. The update also fixes an edge case where bundled frameworks or libraries inside fakesigned and AppSync-installed apps failed to load.

  • Fixes apps crashing on launch a few hours after the jailbreak was applied.
  • Fixes some bundled frameworks and libraries failing to load in fakesigned or AppSync apps.
  • Fixes patchfinder and offset issues on A9 and A10 devices running iOS 13.
  • Fixes A8X support.
  • Improves the kernel exploit success rate on A9 devices.
  • Fixes some tweaks failing to load and includes assorted interface improvements.

These points are the developer’s release notes, not independent device testing. The notes also warn that users of Sonar need an updated Sonar build alongside this release.

Who the update is for

The project describes Amethyst as an iOS 12/13 jailbreak for all devices, while the linked iOS CFW Guide defines the exact firmware range as iOS 12.0 through iOS 13.7. That includes older chip generations specifically named in the v1.1 fixes, but it does not include iOS 14 or later.

Version 1.1 is especially relevant to A8X owners who could not use the earlier build correctly, A9 users seeking better exploit reliability, and A9/A10 users on iOS 13 affected by patchfinder or offset problems. Anyone seeing delayed app-launch crashes or missing tweak injection after jailbreaking also has a concrete reason to update.

Semi-untethered behavior and re-signing

Amethyst remains semi-untethered. After a full reboot, the device returns to a non-jailbroken state until the exploit is applied again. The official guide says that A11-or-newer devices, and any device on iOS 13.0 or later, need the Amethyst app re-signed every seven days when installed with a free Apple Account.

For A10(X) and earlier devices on iOS 12.0–12.5.8, the guide documents a browser-based TotallyNotSpyware-v2 re-jailbreak path after Amethyst has first been installed normally. That web route does not perform the initial installation and does not apply to A11-or-later hardware or iOS 13.

What the fixes do not mean

The new A8X, A9 and A10 work does not extend Amethyst to modern iOS releases. It refines support inside the existing iOS 12.0–13.7 window. Likewise, the AppSync-related fix does not make unsigned applications permanently signed; it targets library and framework loading inside already fakesigned apps.

Amethyst installs Sileo and Zebra according to the official guide. If you are deciding between those package managers after jailbreaking, see our Sileo versus Zebra comparison. For a separate iOS 12.5.6–12.5.8 option, our unc0ver 8.1.0 report explains that project’s current range.

Safe update checklist

  1. Confirm the device is running iOS 12.0–13.7 in Settings → General → About.
  2. Use the official v1.1 GitHub release; avoid mirrored IPA files with unverifiable changes.
  3. If Sonar is installed, update it as the Amethyst release notes require.
  4. Back up package sources and installed tweaks before changing a jailbreak environment.
  5. After a reboot, remember that Amethyst must be re-applied because it is semi-untethered.

For package-manager preparation, use our Sileo backup guide. It does not replace a full device backup, but it helps preserve a record of repositories and packages.

Bottom line

Amethyst v1.1 is a meaningful maintenance release for legacy iPhones and iPads because it fixes failures users can actually feel: delayed app crashes, inconsistent A9 exploitation, A8X support, iOS 13 offsets, and missing tweak or fakesigned-library loading. Its scope is deliberately narrow and well documented: all devices on iOS 12.0–13.7, with semi-untethered behavior unchanged.

Frequently asked questions

Does Amethyst v1.1 support iOS 14 or later?

No. The official project and installation guide list iOS 12.0 through iOS 13.7.

Does v1.1 make Amethyst untethered?

No. Amethyst remains semi-untethered, so a reboot requires re-applying the jailbreak.

Which devices receive targeted fixes?

The release specifically mentions A8X support, better A9 exploit success, and iOS 13 patchfinder and offset fixes for A9 and A10 devices.

Do Sonar users need another update?

Yes. The developer states that Sonar must also be updated for the tweak-loading fix.
