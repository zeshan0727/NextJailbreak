Dopamine 3 Compatibility: Supported iOS Versions by Device Category

Dopamine 3 compatibility is divided into three documented device categories. The 3.x README lists arm64e for iOS 15.0 through iOS 17.3.1, A12 and A13 for iOS 15.0 through iOS 18.7.1 plus a separate iOS 26.0 through iOS 26.0.1 range, and arm64 for iOS 15.0 through iOS 18.7.1. These ranges should be checked separately rather than treated as one universal iOS range.

  • A12 and A13 are documented from iOS 15.0 through iOS 18.7.1, plus a separate iOS 26.0 through iOS 26.0.1 range.
  • arm64 is documented from iOS 15.0 through iOS 18.7.1.

How to interpret iOS shorthand

Broad labels such as iOS 15, iOS 16, and iOS 17 should always be read with the relevant architecture or chip-family condition. A major-version label does not replace the documented endpoint.

The label iOS 18 applies only to categories whose documented range reaches iOS 18.7.1: A12/A13 and arm64. It does not establish universal iOS 18 compatibility, because arm64e has a separate endpoint at iOS 17.3.1.

The label iOS 26 is narrower. The supplied facts document iOS 26.0 through iOS 26.0.1 for A12 and A13 only.

  • Pair every iOS version with arm64e, A12/A13, or arm64.
  • Do not extend iOS 17 arm64e support beyond iOS 17.3.1.
  • Do not treat iOS 18 as universal across device categories.
  • Do not extend the A12/A13 iOS 26 range beyond iOS 26.0.1.

The official website identifies Sileo and Zebra as package managers associated with Dopamine.
