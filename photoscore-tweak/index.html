PhotosCore 1.1.0: Customize Apple Photos Layouts, Navigation, and Albums

PhotosCore 1.1.0 is a jailbreak tweak listed for the iphoneos-arm64 architecture. Its supplied description lists controls for Apple Photos viewing, navigation, album organization, thumbnail layouts, Search items, People and Places, and iPad sidebar destinations and groups. The package lists MobileSubstrate, PreferenceLoader, and firmware version 15.0 or newer as dependencies.

What PhotosCore changes

  • Hides the Photos bottom bar, centers fullscreen photos, sorts albums by name, and opens albums at the bottom.
  • Applies a dark appearance only inside Apple Photos without changing other apps.
  • Offers native thumbnails, a 1–9 column preset, and custom thumbnail layouts with configurable levels, columns, and spacing.
  • Lets you show, hide, and reorder items in Search.
  • Controls whether People and Places appear in Albums.
  • Provides customization for Sidebar destinations and groups on iPad.
