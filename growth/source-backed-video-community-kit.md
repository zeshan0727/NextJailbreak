## Developer-community participation standards

- Contribute original technical findings, reproducible test steps, corrected documentation, responsible issue reports, or a clearly useful comparison first. Add a Next Jailbreak link only when it genuinely answers the question and community rules permit it.
- Link directly to upstream developers as primary sources and clearly identify your relationship to Next Jailbreak.
- Avoid promotional link drops, copy-paste replies, artificial engagement, purchased backlinks, and claims of independent endorsement.
- For GitHub project discussions, prefer upstream changelogs and documentation. Never open unsolicited issues solely to advertise the site.
- Keep a manual outreach register: date, community, question or issue, substantive contribution, optional link, and outcome. Respect moderator decisions.
