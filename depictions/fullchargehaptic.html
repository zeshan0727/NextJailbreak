Free utility tweak

Full Charge Haptic

Receive one success haptic when your iPhone first reaches a full charge while connected to power.

Compatibility: iOS 15 and later
Builds: RootHide and standard rootless
Settings: One enable or disable switch
Package: com.nextsolution.fullchargehaptic

The tweak ignores initial state, duplicate full-charge notifications, unplugged 100% readings and disabled-state events.
