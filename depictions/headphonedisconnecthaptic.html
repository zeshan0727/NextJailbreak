Headphone Disconnect Haptic

One focused warning haptic when wired or Bluetooth headphones disconnect and audio falls back away from a personal audio route.

Version 1.0.0iOS 15+RootHideRootless

How it behaves

The tweak reacts only to an actual old-device-unavailable audio-route event. Connecting headphones, ordinary audio-session category changes and switching directly between personal-audio outputs do not produce the warning.

Settings

A single Enable Disconnect Alert switch is available in Settings. No diagnostic or developer controls are included in the release pane.

Compatibility

Built for iOS 15 and later, with RootHide iphoneos-arm64e and standard rootless iphoneos-arm64 variants. Physical-device confirmation is still required after installation.
