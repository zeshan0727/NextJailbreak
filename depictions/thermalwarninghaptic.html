Thermal Warning Haptic

One focused warning haptic when iOS newly enters a serious or critical thermal state.

Version 1.0.0iOS 15+RootHideRootless

How it behaves

The tweak uses Apple’s thermal-state change notification and alerts only when the state crosses from nominal or fair into serious or critical. Startup state, recovery transitions, repeated states and serious-to-critical escalation do not buzz again.

Settings

A single Enable Thermal Alert switch is available in Settings. No diagnostic or developer controls are included in the release pane.

Compatibility

Built for iOS 15 and later, with RootHide iphoneos-arm64e and standard rootless iphoneos-arm64 variants. Physical-device confirmation is still required after installation.
