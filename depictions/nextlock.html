NextLock
Lock Screen, your way.
by Next Jailbreak
Version 1.1.4 • Rootless & RootHide • iOS 15+

A focused Lock Screen customization tweak for time, date and icons with precise styling, positioning and shadows.

What you can customize

Time
Size, color, 27 fonts, weight, style, alignment, X/Y position and shadow.
Date
Independent appearance, formatting, alignment, positioning and shadow.
Icon
30+ SF Symbols, color, size, anchor, placement, offsets and shadow.
Custom Photo
Normal photos appear as rounded squares; transparent PNGs keep a sticker-style shape.

Version 1.1.4

Custom Photo now supports four simultaneous frames. Frame 1 preserves the existing photo setup and Frames 2–4 can be enabled separately. Every frame has independent photo selection, 20–600pt width and height, Time/Date anchor, placement and X/Y offsets.

Clean Settings design

NextLock separates Time, Date, Icon, Custom Photo, Advanced and About into dedicated pages so the tweak stays easy to navigate without removing advanced controls.

Live preference reload remains available, with quick reset controls for individual sections.
