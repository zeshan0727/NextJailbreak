Brightness Edge Haptic

A small iOS 15–16 jailbreak tweak that gives tactile confirmation when display brightness newly reaches its minimum or maximum edge.

Feature

Warning haptic at minimum brightness and success haptic at maximum brightness. Repeated brightness notifications at the same edge are ignored.

Compatibility

RootHide and standard rootless builds. Primary test target: iPhone 14 Pro Max on iOS 16.0.

Settings

One Enable Brightness Edge Haptic switch in the Settings app.
