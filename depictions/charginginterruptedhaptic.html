Charging Interrupted Haptic

A focused convenience tweak that plays one warning haptic when your iPhone transitions from charging or full to unplugged.

iOS 15+RootHideRootlessFree

Why it helps

Notice a loose cable, interrupted wireless charging session, or accidental disconnection without repeatedly checking the screen.

Settings

One clean Enable Charging Alert switch in the Settings app.
