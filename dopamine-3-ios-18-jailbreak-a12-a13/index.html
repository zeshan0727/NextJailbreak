Dopamine 3 Jailbreak: iOS 18 Compatibility for A12 and A13 Devices

Dopamine 3.x iOS 18 compatibility depends on the device category listed in the official README. A12 and A13 devices are documented from iOS 15.0 through iOS 18.7.1, so iOS 18.0 falls within that range. The general arm64e listing is separate and ends at iOS 17.3.1, so the A12/A13 iOS 18 range must not be generalized to every arm64e device.
