Remove Widget Background 2.1.1: Home Screen Widget Background Removal

Remove Widget Background version 2.1.1 is a Home Screen tweak by 82flex. Its listed function is to remove the background of widgets on the Home Screen. The release is marked as updated from version 2.0 and has package variants for iphoneos-arm and iphoneos-arm64.
