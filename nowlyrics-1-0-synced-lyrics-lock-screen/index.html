NowLyrics 1.1.2 Fixes Dynamic Island Lyrics and Improves Musixmatch Matching

The update tightens lyric timing across music apps, prevents Dynamic Island collisions, and makes Musixmatch choose the recording version actually playing.

Originally published October 1, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · Verified from the current official Havoc listing

NowLyrics 1.1.2 was released on October 7, 2026. The $2.99 rootless tweak keeps line-synced lyrics visible across the Lock Screen, Control Center, Dynamic Island and CarPlay while addressing timing, layout and settings problems reported after the original 1.0 release.

Compatibility boundary: Havoc’s package header advertises iOS 15.0–26.0.1 and rootless support. The longer developer description also discusses iOS 26.1 testing. Treat 26.0.1 as the marketplace’s current formal range, and remember that tweak compatibility does not mean a rootless jailbreak exists for every device and firmware in that range.

What changed in NowLyrics 1.1.2

  • The settings page now works on iOS 18 and later under Settings › General; it requires PreferenceLoader 2.2.8.
  • Musixmatch matching now selects lyrics for the recording being played—such as the single, album or radio edit—instead of relying only on a broad song match.
  • Dynamic Island lyrics no longer shake sideways on iOS 17 and later and now make room when Face ID or another system activity uses the island.
  • Long lines retain their first and last letters at the faded edges.
  • Spotify and other players update the lyric at the correct time instead of roughly half a second late.
  • The update fixes occasional lyric jumps, track-title flashes, missing Settings icons on older iOS versions and a rare settings-change crash inside music apps.

Where NowLyrics displays lyrics

NowLyrics replaces the title field in system now-playing surfaces rather than drawing a separate full-screen lyrics view. Its supported paths include Spotify and Apple Music, plus YouTube Music, KKBOX, SoundCloud, TIDAL, Deezer and Amazon Music through system now-playing information. Other players can be selected from the tweak’s searchable app list if they publish compatible metadata.

Lyrics sources and privacy

LRCLIB and NetEase Cloud Music work without an account. Musixmatch requires the user’s own token or API key, but offers the largest catalogue. NowLyrics lets one source take priority and falls back to the others when necessary. A manually supplied .lrc file in the app’s Library/NowLyrics/manual/ folder takes precedence over online results.

The developer says only the track title, artist, album and duration are sent to lyric services. A Musixmatch token stays on the device and is sent only to Musixmatch; the tweak does not require an account and declares no analytics.

Settings, offsets and known limits

  • Timing can be shifted from −3 to +3 seconds, with an exact value such as 0.25 or -300ms.
  • Fetched lyrics are cached per app and can be cleared from settings.
  • Settings are localized in English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and Japanese.
  • Title, artist and duration matching can miss live recordings, remasters and rare versions; manual LRC files are the fallback.
  • NetEase and Musixmatch access relies on unofficial services that may change or stop working.
  • NowLyrics cannot be installed alongside AMLyrics because both modify the same Apple Music item.

On iOS 18 and later, install PreferenceLoader 2.2.8 from dhinakg’s repository, then find NowLyrics at the bottom of Settings › General. The developer also instructs users to tap Apply once after installation for the Dynamic Island feature.

Should existing users update?

Version 1.1.2 is a meaningful maintenance update if you use Spotify, Musixmatch or Dynamic Island lyrics. Its fixes target delayed lines, visual collisions and incorrect recording matches rather than adding a new lyrics surface. Users on older iOS versions also receive the settings-icon and crash fixes.

Frequently asked questions

Does NowLyrics provide word-by-word lyrics?

No. The current sources supply line-synced LRC lyrics.

Does the advertised iOS range guarantee a jailbreak?

No. NowLyrics still requires a compatible rootless jailbreak for the exact device and firmware.

Can NowLyrics and AMLyrics be installed together?

No. The developer says both rewrite the same Apple Music item.
