NowLyrics 1.3.1 Adds Word-by-Word Control Center Lyrics

The update turns Control Center into a configurable lyrics surface, adds dedicated lyric modules, and builds on version 1.3.0’s listening history and timing fixes.

Originally published October 1, 2026 · Updated November 26, 2026 · Verified from the current official Havoc listing

NowLyrics 1.3.1 was released on November 25, 2026. The $2.99 tweak adds configurable word-by-word lyrics to Control Center media players and introduces dedicated NowLyrics modules in sizes from 1×1 through 3×4. It is a substantial interface update, not just a small compatibility patch.

Compatibility boundary: Havoc lists NowLyrics 1.3.1 as a rootless package for iOS 15.0–26.0.1. The new Control Center player behavior is specifically documented for iOS 16 and iOS 26. That package range does not mean a compatible rootless jailbreak exists for every device and firmware inside it, and the listing does not claim RootHide support.

What changed in NowLyrics 1.3.1

  • Control Center’s media player can place lyrics below the cover or beside it, with the current word highlighted and the remaining words dimmed.
  • On iOS 16, supported media-player sizes are 1×4, 2×2, 2×4 and 3×4; a closed player begins showing lyrics at 2×4.
  • Dedicated NowLyrics Control Center modules are available from 1×1 through 3×4, and users can add more than one module.
  • A new Control Center settings page controls player size, lyrics while closed or open, lyric sources in the output selector and whether the player sits over the cover.
  • Off for This Song suppresses lyrics for the current track until a source is selected again.

What version 1.3.0 added first

Version 1.3.0, released November 1, laid the groundwork for this update. Its optional on-device Listening History can retain 30 days, 90 days, one year or all history, with import and export controls. It also added an “Always show” Lyrics Island option for iPads and devices using a tweak or MobileGestalt change to expose Dynamic Island.

The same release moved line timing about 0.3 seconds earlier for smoother fades while keeping word highlighting on the exact timestamp. It also corrected Spotify Bluetooth delay synchronization, source-switch races, track-transition timing and Apple Music retries, and reduced the tweak’s size and memory use according to the developer changelog.

Lyrics sources, apps and privacy

NowLyrics supports LRCLIB, NetEase Cloud Music and Musixmatch; Musixmatch requires the user’s own token or API key. It can use Apple Music and Spotify directly, while YouTube Music, KKBOX, SoundCloud, TIDAL, Deezer, Amazon Music and other players depend on compatible system now-playing metadata. A manual .lrc file in the app’s Library/NowLyrics/manual/ folder takes priority over online results.

The developer says track title, artist, album and duration are sent to lyric services. A Musixmatch token stays on the device and is sent only to Musixmatch; the listing declares no analytics. Listening History is optional and stored locally.

Before and after updating

  1. Confirm the package manager offers 1.3.1 from Havoc and that the device already has a compatible rootless jailbreak.
  2. Remove or disable AMLyrics first. The developer says it conflicts because both tweaks modify the same Apple Music item.
  3. On iOS 18 or later, make sure PreferenceLoader 2.2.8 or newer is installed, then open NowLyrics under Settings › General.
  4. After upgrading, tap Apply once and respring. Re-add or resize the Control Center player/module if its layout does not refresh.
  5. If lyrics are early or late, test without a Bluetooth route, then use the per-app timing offset. If only one track is blank, check that “Off for This Song” is not selected and switch the lyrics source.
  6. If SpringBoard enters Safe Mode, disable NowLyrics and any competing music-player tweak, respring, and re-enable one at a time.

Should existing users update?

Yes if Control Center is your main playback surface or you want word-level progress without opening a music app. Users who rely on Bluetooth audio also gain the earlier 1.3.0 timing correction. If the existing Lock Screen lyric line already works and you do not use Control Center modules, the update is less urgent—but it still contains source, transition and Apple Music reliability fixes.

Download only through the official NowLyrics Havoc page. The official page is also the authority for the current version, price, package range and changelog.

Frequently asked questions

Does NowLyrics 1.3.1 provide word-by-word lyrics?

Yes. The documented iOS 16 and iOS 26 Control Center layouts can highlight the current word and dim the others when the selected lyrics source supplies word timestamps.

Does the advertised iOS range guarantee a jailbreak?

No. NowLyrics still requires a compatible rootless jailbreak for the exact device and firmware.

Does the listing confirm RootHide support?

No. Havoc marks the package rootless; it does not list a separate RootHide build.

Can NowLyrics and AMLyrics be installed together?

No. The developer says both modify the same Apple Music item.
