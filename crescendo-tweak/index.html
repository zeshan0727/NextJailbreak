Crescendo 1.1 Adds a Volume Slider to the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

Crescendo 1.1 is a Tweaks package by Yves in the Lock Screen category. Its description states that it adds a volume slider to the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island. The release lists iphoneos-arm and iphoneos-arm64 variants, with ElleKit or MobileSubstrate version 0.9.5000 or later, firmware version 16.0 or later, and PreferenceLoader as dependencies.
