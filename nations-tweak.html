Nations 1.0: Lock-Screen Audio Spectrum Tweak Explained

Nations is a lock-screen tweak attributed to uzra and cataloged as version 1.0 for the iphoneos-arm64 architecture. Its supplied description states: “Trap Nation-style audio spectrum on lock screen!” The package is listed in the Tweaks section under the Lock Screen category and carries a commercial tag. The release lists MobileSubstrate, PreferenceLoader, and com.ryannair05.audiosnapshotserver2 as dependencies. The supplied facts do not identify an iOS version, device model, jailbreak tool, configuration interface, or post-installation procedure.
