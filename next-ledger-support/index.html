Next Ledger Support

Next Ledger is a personal ledger and budgeting utility for iPhone. It helps you record income, expenses and transfers, organize accounts and categories, review reports, set budgets, and create backups.

Getting started

  • Create one or more accounts and choose their currencies.
  • Add income, expenses, or transfers from the Home or Transactions screens.
  • Use Reports to review your recorded activity and spending summaries.
  • Use Settings to export CSV/JSON files or enable optional iCloud Drive backup.
  • Use Apple Shortcuts if you want to create personal data-entry automations.

Privacy

Your ledger records are stored locally unless you intentionally use a backup or export feature. Next Ledger only accesses files and locations that you choose through Apple's system interfaces.
