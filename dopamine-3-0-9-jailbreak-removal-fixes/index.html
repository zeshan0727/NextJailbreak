Dopamine 3.0.11 Restores iOS 26 Beta 1 Support and Fixes Boot-Logo Resprings

Version 3.0.11 is a focused maintenance release: it reverses an iOS 26.0 beta 1 regression, prevents a boot-logo framebuffer freeze during userspace resprings, and refreshes two low-level injection components.

By Zeeshan Barvi · Originally published September 22, 2026 · Updated October 13, 2026 · Version 3.0.11 published October 12, 2026

What Dopamine 3.0.11 changes

opa334 published Dopamine 3.0.11 on October 12, 2026 at 22:56 UTC. The official changelog lists three changes:

  • iOS 26.0 beta 1 support is fixed. The developer identifies this as a regression introduced in Dopamine 3.0.1.
  • The boot logo is cleaned before backboardd starts. Previously, the framebuffer could remain stuck on its last content when a userspace respring (sbreload) followed a boot-logo display. The new behavior fades the display to black first.
  • opainject and litehook are updated. The release notes do not specify separate user-facing features for these component refreshes, so users should not infer a new exploit or broader device support from that line alone.

Who should install this update?

The most direct beneficiaries are users on supported A12 or A13 devices running iOS 26.0 beta 1, and anyone who has seen the display appear frozen on the boot logo or previous framebuffer content during a userspace respring. Even if neither case applies, 3.0.11 is the current official build and supersedes 3.0.10.

This is a maintenance update, not a new jailbreak method. It does not change Dopamine’s semi-untethered model: after a full reboot, you still need to open Dopamine and jailbreak again. Our Dopamine re-jailbreak guide explains that workflow.

Compatibility: iOS 26 support is not universal

The project’s 3.0.11 README documents separate hardware ranges. Do not treat “iOS 26 support” as compatibility with every iPhone or iPad:

  • arm64e devices: iOS 15.0–17.3.1.
  • A12 and A13 devices: iOS 15.0–18.7.1, plus iOS 26.0–26.0.1.
  • arm64 devices: iOS 15.0–18.7.1.

Those are jailbreak availability ranges from the official project, not merely package metadata. Before updating or installing tweaks, use the jailbreak and tweak compatibility checklist. For the wider Dopamine 3 status and setup context, see our Dopamine 3 hub.

Recent Dopamine 3 release history

  • 3.0.11 — October 12, 2026: restores iOS 26.0 beta 1 support, fixes the boot-logo framebuffer transition during sbreload, and updates opainject and litehook.
  • 3.0.10 — September 23, 2026: added a memory-hooking API used by current Frida builds and fixed an Xcode debugging workflow after userspace reboot on iOS 17 and newer.
  • 3.0.9 — September 22, 2026: improved jailbreak-removal cleanup and related maintenance behavior.

Keeping these changes on one canonical page avoids presenting each maintenance release as a separate jailbreak. The version number in the Dopamine app should read 3.0.11 after a successful update.

How to download safely

  1. Open the official Dopamine 3.0.11 release.
  2. Choose the official Dopamine.ipa or Dopamine.tipa asset appropriate to your installation workflow.
  3. If you verify downloads, both official assets are listed with SHA-256 fbd5b9086634cac0b6c7e5ba95c43d8c40ecafd814f8e620beb06f355480b973.
  4. Do not use repackaged builds or third-party mirrors. Next Jailbreak does not host the binaries.

Updating the app alone does not expand the supported firmware range. If you already use Dopamine, keep your mobile and root credentials secure; our Dopamine password guide covers the recommended separation.

Bottom line

Dopamine 3.0.11 is a worthwhile maintenance update because it fixes a concrete regression and a visible respring problem without changing the project’s documented jailbreak model. The official notes do not claim a new exploit, universal iOS 26 coverage, or a broad performance gain.
