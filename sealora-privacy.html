Sealora Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Sealora is an iOS application for importing, locally signing, storing and exporting IPA files using signing credentials you provide. This policy describes how data is handled by Sealora's application features, advertising integration and optional installation handoff.

Signing credentials and app files

Imported IPA files and generated signed files are retained in the app's local storage. Signing certificates, private keys and passwords are processed locally and stored using iOS-protected storage and Keychain where supported. Sealora does not send these signing assets to an external signing service as part of the signing operation. You can manage or delete imported files and signed outputs within the app.

Advertising

When banner advertisements are enabled, Sealora integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and, where applicable, Google's User Messaging Platform for regional privacy choices. Google and its partners may process device information, app interactions, advertising identifiers (subject to device permissions and applicable law), network identifiers, and diagnostic data according to their policies. Sealora does not transmit your imported IPA contents, certificate private keys, or signing passwords to Google for ad requests. You can disable the banner in Settings. If required by your region, an advertising privacy choices control appears in Settings.

For Google's disclosures and controls, see Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob.

Installation handoff

Where direct OTA installation is used, Sealora may request an HTTPS installation manifest from a third-party manifest service. The service can receive the app bundle identifier, display name, version and local loopback IPA URL used to prepare the install manifest. The signed IPA is normally transferred from the device's local server to iOS during that process. The third-party service may process ordinary network request data, such as your IP address. The installation flow does not intentionally send signing certificates or their passwords.

Data control, security and retention

Signing files and credentials remain available locally until deleted or removed by the operating system, subject to device protection and backup settings. Removing the app can delete local files. Third-party services follow their own retention policies. Protect and back up your signed files as appropriate. No online account is required for local signing.

Children and geographic restrictions

Sealora is a development utility and is not designed for children. Availability of certain advertising services and privacy choices may depend on your location, device settings, and current Google requirements.
