SleepSaver 2 1.4 Adds Brightness Control and Gapless Playlists

SleepSaver 2 version 1.4 improves the parts of a video-screensaver setup users notice every day: consistent brightness, transitions between playlist items and coexistence with the Emerald tweak. The official update arrived on October 20, 2026.

By Zeeshan BarviPublished October 21, 20266 min read
Release snapshot: Havoc lists SleepSaver 2 version 1.4 at $2.49, released October 20, 2026, for iOS 14.0–26.0.1 with jailbreak and rootless support. The release adds brightness control, gapless playlists and improved Emerald compatibility.

What changed in SleepSaver 2 1.4

The official 1.4 changelog contains three specific changes:

  • An option to play screensavers at a chosen brightness level.
  • Gapless playback when moving between videos in a playlist.
  • Improved compatibility with the Emerald tweak.

These are practical refinements rather than a redesign. Version 1.4 keeps the existing idea—play a selected video when the device auto-locks—while giving users better control over how bright that video appears and how smoothly multiple clips follow one another.

Brightness control is separate from video brightness

The new setting lets SleepSaver 2 apply a specific display brightness while the screensaver is playing. That is useful when a source video was mastered too brightly for a dark room or too dimly for daytime viewing. It also avoids editing every source clip just to make the display comfortable.

Start conservatively. A bright, continuously lit display consumes more energy than allowing the screen to turn off, regardless of the tweak’s other battery-saving options. On OLED devices, dark footage can reduce the number of brightly illuminated pixels, but it does not make an active video free of battery cost.

Gapless playlist playback

Before 1.4, switching between selected clips could expose a visible pause or transition. The changelog now promises gapless playlist playback, so consecutive videos should feel like one screensaver sequence rather than separate launches.

The official page does not define whether “gapless” removes every frame of transition across every codec. If a particular playlist still flashes or pauses, test the same clip alone and then test a playlist made from videos with matching resolution, orientation and frame rate. That separates a source-format issue from a playlist-transition issue.

Emerald compatibility

The developer also lists improved compatibility with Emerald. No additional technical detail is published in the 1.4 changelog, so it would be inaccurate to promise a specific conflict has been eliminated. Users running both tweaks should update SleepSaver 2, respring, and verify their normal Emerald and screensaver behavior together.

What SleepSaver 2 already offers

SleepSaver 2 can use videos from the Photos library or its included presets. Playback controls include zoom-to-fill, fade effects, iPad rotation, shuffled playlists, swipe-to-skip, playback speed from 0.25× to 2× and separate loop limits for battery and charging.

Its activation rules are the more important part for daily use. The official listing offers controls to suppress playback during Do Not Disturb, on the Lock Screen, in Low Power Mode, while on battery, while music is playing, after a notification wake, or below a chosen battery level. It can also dismiss on tap, device raise, notification or call.

Compatibility needs two checks

Havoc currently lists iOS 14.0–26.0.1 and marks rootless support. That is the package’s compatibility range, not a universal jailbreak matrix. The device and exact firmware still need a compatible jailbreak, working tweak injection and an environment that supports the package.

In particular, the presence of iOS 26.0.1 on the package page does not mean every iPhone on that firmware can be jailbroken. Confirm jailbreak availability first, then use the package range to judge whether SleepSaver 2 supports that environment.

Recommended update steps

  1. Refresh Havoc in Sileo or Zebra and confirm version 1.4 is shown.
  2. Record the current activation conditions, playlist and loop settings.
  3. Review the package-manager queue before installing; do not use unofficial mirrors.
  4. Install and respring.
  5. Set a moderate screensaver brightness, then test one video before rebuilding a playlist.
  6. If Emerald is installed, test its normal interface immediately after the SleepSaver check.
  7. Watch battery use over a normal day before increasing brightness or loop counts.

The safe Sileo installation guide covers queue review and recovery. If SpringBoard enters Safe Mode, capture a useful report with the KrashKop crash-log guide and include the active playlist format and other Lock Screen tweaks.

Who should update?

Users with several videos in one playlist benefit most directly from gapless playback. Anyone who found the previous screensaver too bright or too dim can now solve that inside the tweak instead of modifying the video. Emerald users also have a documented reason to move to 1.4.

If version 1.3.1 is stable and none of those cases applies, 1.4 is still the current official build, but the public changelog does not claim unrelated crash fixes. Update through Havoc when convenient and keep the existing configuration available for comparison.

Frequently asked questions

What is new in SleepSaver 2 version 1.4?

A specific screensaver brightness setting, gapless playlist playback and improved compatibility with Emerald.

Which iOS versions are listed?

Havoc lists iOS 14.0–26.0.1. A compatible jailbreak for the exact device and firmware is still required.

Does it support rootless jailbreaks?

Yes. The current Havoc package information marks rootless support.

Will a video screensaver affect battery life?

An active display and video playback consume power. SleepSaver 2 offers activation limits, loop controls, Low Power Mode conditions and the new brightness setting to help manage that cost.
