Regram 6.5 vs Rocket 4.25.0: Which Instagram Tweak Should You Use?

Regram 6.5 is the deeper choice for follow-request history, Messages Only scheduling, media watching and multiple official installation paths. Rocket 4.25.0 is the clearer choice for iPhone Duo and explicitly documented rootful, rootless and RootHide packages.

By Zeeshan BarviPublished October 18, 2026 · Updated October 30, 20269 min read
Short answer: choose Regram for the broadest Instagram workflow and tracking toolkit, especially if you want an official TrollStore or sideload build. Choose Rocket when jailbreak-package clarity, iPhone Duo layouts or a more focused privacy/download feature set matters more. Neither package makes a jailbreak available for unsupported hardware or firmware.

What changed since the previous comparison?

Regram 6.5 adds Instagram 451/452 compatibility and fixes a Reels Limit crash on Instagram 452. Its larger additions are an independent Messages Only settings page with schedules, Shortcuts links and an optional Profile tab; received follow-request tracking; stronger tracking-parameter removal; and more reliable Media Watch saving and bulk cleanup.

Rocket 4.25.0 supersedes 4.24.0. It adds iPhone Duo support through adaptive grids, tab customization and revised Rocket-button placement, while explicitly adding Instagram 452.0.1 compatibility. These changes materially affect the device and host-app advice in the earlier version of this page.

Which has better privacy and messaging controls?

Regram wins on workflow depth. Messages Only can now be scheduled by time and day, switched immediately or after confirmation, opened through Shortcuts and exposed as an optional tab. Its official feature page also documents manual seen controls, retained unsent messages, typing and screenshot-notification controls, and local activity history.

Rocket covers the high-value essentials: Story and DM seen behavior, typing and screenshot behavior, unlimited Instants replay, local retention of received messages after unsending, and call recording. Rocket is easier to explain, while Regram provides more automation and local records. Those records may contain sensitive account activity, so review storage and consent implications before enabling them.

Follow requests, profiles and watched media

Regram 6.5 can warn before sending a new follow request to someone who previously rejected one. Its new Received tab records incoming requests and whether they were approved, declined or withdrawn, with repeat-request markers and filters. Media Watch can retry failed saves, select all media and delete saved media across accounts.

Rocket offers profile watching, bulk profile-post downloads, cache cleanup and account-specific downloads with previews, filters, retries and bulk deletion. Choose Regram if follow-request history and detailed watchlists are central. Choose Rocket if you mainly want automatic media collection and a conventional download manager.

Reels, Stories and downloads

Regram remains stronger for granular playback: its documented tools include Reels and Story speed controls, scrubbing, skipping, Picture in Picture, frame saving, limiting consecutive Reels and manual seen actions. Version 6.5 fixes a crash when opening a feed Reel with Reels Limit enabled and can hide Instagram’s new Shop Similar button.

Rocket supports media downloads and reposting, Reels Picture in Picture, auto-scroll, disabling infinite scrolling and private Story viewing. Version 4.24.0 added a switch for Reels friend suggestions; 4.25.0 retains that feature while adding iPhone Duo layouts. Both can cover everyday saving and privacy needs, but Regram exposes more playback-level controls.

iPhone Duo and interface customization

Rocket is the verified iPhone Duo choice. Its 4.25.0 changelog specifically names iPhone Duo, adaptive grid layouts, tab customization and button placement. Regram’s 6.5 official notes do not make a matching iPhone Duo claim, so this page does not infer support from generic iOS or Instagram compatibility.

On conventional iPhones, both provide substantial customization. Regram offers custom tabs, long-press destinations, menu editors and extensive per-feature filtering. Rocket supports list/grid feeds, custom themes, fonts, icons, tab layouts and per-account settings profiles.

Compatibility and installation are still the deciding factors

Regram’s official repository documents jailbreak installation plus four current Instagram 452.0 variants: regular, no-plugins, duplicate and TrollStore. Its dedicated TrollStore guide currently lists a 6.5 build based on Instagram 452.0.1 and warns that this TIPA crashes if installed with AltStore or a signing service. Use the build made for your installer; do not interchange them.

Rocket’s package page requires iOS 15.0 or later and the App Store Instagram app. Its official repository says one APT source serves rootful, rootless and RootHide builds. That makes Rocket easier to select inside Sileo or Zebra, but “iOS 15+” is package compatibility—not proof that your device and firmware have a working jailbreak.

If you are not jailbroken, Regram publishes complete prebuilt variants with bundle and notification tradeoffs. Rocket provides a tweak source for sideloading tools and says it does not distribute a full Instagram IPA. For broader installer differences, see the TrollStore vs SideStore vs AltStore comparison.

Practical recommendation

  • Choose Regram for Messages Only schedules, follow-request records, deep playback, local tracking and an official TrollStore or sideload build.
  • Choose Rocket for iPhone Duo, explicit rootful/rootless/RootHide packages and a focused privacy/download/customization toolkit.
  • Choose neither yet if your Instagram version, package architecture, signing method or jailbreak environment is unclear.

Do not install both together unless both developers explicitly document coexistence. Close Instagram before changing packages, export settings where supported, and use only the official repositories. The Regram 6.5 update page provides Regram-specific release details; Rocket 4.25.0 details are linked directly from the official sources below.

Troubleshooting before switching

  1. Record the installed Instagram version and tweak build.
  2. Confirm whether the package manager labels the package rootful, rootless or RootHide.
  3. Disable or remove the other Instagram injection tweak; do not troubleshoot two injectors at once.
  4. Launch the unmodified App Store Instagram once after an update, then reinstall only the compatible tweak build.
  5. If a feature disappeared, compare the official changelog with your host-app version before downgrading Instagram.

Frequently asked questions

Which tweak supports Instagram 452?

Both current official releases target the Instagram 452 generation. Regram’s general build table lists 452.0 and its TrollStore guide lists 452.0.1; Rocket 4.25.0 explicitly adds 452.0.1 compatibility.

Which is better for RootHide?

Rocket is the clearer recommendation because its official repository explicitly provides a RootHide build. Check Regram’s exact package architecture before installing rather than assuming compatibility from an iOS range.

Which is better for iPhone Duo?

Rocket 4.25.0 is the source-supported choice because its changelog explicitly adds adaptive iPhone Duo layouts and button placement. Regram 6.5 does not make the same public claim.

Can I install both?

The official sources checked do not promise coexistence. Since both inject into Instagram, test one at a time and remove the other before diagnosing crashes or missing controls.
