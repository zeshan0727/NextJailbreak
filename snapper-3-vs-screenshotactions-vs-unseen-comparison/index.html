Lasso vs Snapper 3 vs Unseen: Which Screenshot Tweak Should You Use?

Lasso and Snapper 3 both crop and pin captures, but they target different budgets and firmware ranges. Unseen solves a different problem: stopping ordinary apps from reacting to screenshot or recording state.

By Zeeshan Barvi · First published October 21, 2026 · Updated October 29, 2026 after Lasso 1.0.0 launched

Quick verdict

  • Choose Lasso if you want the broadest all-in-one toolkit: cropped and scrolling captures, persistent pins, OCR and barcodes, markup, region recording, call/system-audio recording, captions, transcripts and a searchable library.
  • Choose Snapper 3 if you mainly need fast cropping, pinning, OCR, QR scanning and capture history on iOS 14–17.2, and prefer a smaller, less expensive package.
  • Choose Unseen if the problem is an app detecting screenshots or screen recording. It is a privacy/control utility, not a capture editor.
Compatibility warning: Havoc’s firmware range describes the package, not jailbreak availability. Your exact device, firmware and jailbreak environment must still support tweak injection. Lasso’s current listing says rootless; it does not claim a RootHide package.

Lasso 1.0.0: the broadest toolkit

Lasso starts from the normal screenshot gesture, freezes the screen and lets you drag across a region. The selected area can be copied, saved, shared, pinned over other apps, read as text, marked up or recorded. Pins can persist after a respring, accept or pass through touches, sit on the Lock Screen, or remain live so a changing timer or score stays visible.

Its biggest difference from Snapper 3 is scope. The official listing adds long scrolling captures, region screen recording, GIF output, system-audio and microphone capture, call recording from Phone, FaceTime and calling apps, live captions, on-device transcripts, translation, Shortcuts actions and a protected library. The listing says text and speech processing stays on-device unless the user shares the result.

That breadth also creates more responsibility. Lasso is the largest and most expensive option here, and call recording may be regulated. Its own feature list offers beep or spoken notices; use them where appropriate and follow the law in your location.

Snapper 3: mature, focused cropping and pins

Snapper 3 remains the clearer purchase when the job is simply to capture a region and keep it visible while working elsewhere. It supports cropped floating captures, copy/share/save actions, OCR, on-screen QR recognition, capture history, Imgur upload and plugins.

It costs less and its narrower feature set may be easier to troubleshoot than an all-in-one recording library. The tradeoff is the official iOS ceiling: Havoc lists iOS 14.0–17.2. Version 1.2.1 improved support for alternative jailbreaks, and the listing says RootHide, Bootstrap and Serotonin “should work”; that wording is not the same as a complete tested matrix.

Unseen: complementary capture-state controls

Unseen does not compete on editing, OCR or pins. Its controls target screenshot events, screen-recording or mirroring state, and app layers that ordinary applications hide during capture. On iOS 17 and later, version 0.0.5 can also prevent selected system interface elements—such as the Dynamic Island or screenshot preview—from appearing in captures.

The developer explicitly says its hidden-content option does not bypass DRM or system copyright protections. Use it for legitimate capture behavior and privacy needs, not as a claim that protected media can be recorded.

What happened to ScreenshotActions?

ScreenshotActions remains a useful free legacy option for users who only want quick actions from Apple’s stock screenshot preview. Its official version is still 1.2 from April 15, 2023, with an iOS 12.0–15.7.5 range. It no longer belongs in the main comparison for current firmware because Lasso now covers preview-to-action workflows and much more through iOS 26. This page keeps the same canonical route to avoid publishing a near-duplicate.

Which should you install?

You want one current package for almost every capture task

Pick Lasso if your compatible rootless jailbreak and budget fit. Its iOS range is the widest here, and no other option combines pins, OCR, markup, long captures, audio, calls and transcripts.

You mainly pin references while working

Pick Snapper 3 on supported firmware. It is cheaper, mature and more focused. For a different screenshot-and-search workflow, compare Prism and Circle to Search.

You only need apps to stop reacting to screenshots

Pick Unseen. Read the site’s Unseen overview for its exact controls and limits. It can complement a capture tool, but running overlapping system hooks increases the chance of conflicts.

Installation and troubleshooting

  1. Check the environment before buying. Match your actual iOS version, device jailbreak and package architecture. “Rootless” on a listing does not make the tweak compatible with every rootless-capable firmware.
  2. Remove or disable overlapping screenshot hooks first. If two packages intercept the screenshot buttons or preview, test one at a time and respring between changes.
  3. Test the basic screenshot gesture before enabling extras. Confirm capture, pin dismissal and the stock preview, then add recording, automation or privacy controls.
  4. If SpringBoard enters Safe Mode, disable the newest package. Use the bad-tweak recovery guide, then collect and share a crash report with the developer using the KrashKop guide.
  5. Protect recordings and transcripts. Lasso’s library may contain sensitive calls, screens and speech. Use its device lock option and remove material you no longer need.
