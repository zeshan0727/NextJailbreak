For a technical report

  • State the device model and exact iOS version.
  • Name the jailbreak environment and package version.
  • Describe the expected result and what actually happened.
  • List the smallest reliable steps that reproduce the issue.
  • Remove personal information from logs and screenshots.

For a correction request

Include the page title, the statement that may be wrong or outdated, and a reliable source or direct test result. Compatibility corrections should specify the exact setup that was tested.

Advertising enquiries should identify the company and proposed placement. Undisclosed paid links or requests to alter editorial conclusions are not accepted.

Support boundaries
