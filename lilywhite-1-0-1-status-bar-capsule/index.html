lilywhite 1.1.0 Adds Dual-SIM Signal Rows and Custom Status Bar Time

Version 1.1.0 gives lilywhite’s unified status-bar capsule more useful information: two signal rows for Dual-SIM devices, 5G/4G/LTE labels away from Wi-Fi, flexible time formatting and control over which apps may show notification icons.

By Zeeshan Barvi · Published October 1, 2026 · Updated October 3, 2026 · Package released October 2, 2026

What changed in lilywhite 1.1.0?

The October 2 release adds six documented changes:

  • Dual-SIM signal rows: both mobile lines can now appear as two rows of dots in the capsule’s lower gap.
  • Network-type labels: 5G, 4G or LTE appears beside the clock when the phone is not connected to Wi-Fi.
  • Flexible time formats: the new setting can add seconds, the date, the weekday or a custom pattern.
  • Notification filtering: you can choose which apps are allowed to place notification icons beside the notch.
  • Control Center cleanup: the update fixes stock signal and battery icons ghosting while Control Center opens or closes.
  • Dynamic Island spacing: lilywhite’s capsule and notification icons move aside when the Dynamic Island expands, including during screen recording.

These are practical additions rather than a compatibility expansion. Havoc still lists iOS 16.0 through 26.0 and rootless support.

What lilywhite changes

lilywhite replaces the separate clock, Wi-Fi, mobile-signal and battery indicators with a compact capsule. The capsule’s outline acts as a battery gauge, the clock and Wi-Fi share the center, and cellular strength is represented by dots along the lower edge. A single status icon can represent Focus, silent mode, location use or screen recording, while up to three notification-app icons appear on the other side of the notch.

Compatibility: check the device shape, not only iOS

Havoc lists iOS 16.0–26.0, a rootless jailbreak requirement and a US$2.49 price. The developer specifically describes Dopamine with ElleKit. The package is intended for iPhones with a notch or Dynamic Island; it stays inactive and preserves the stock status bar on unsupported shapes such as iPad, iPod touch and iPhone SE.

That package range does not mean every device on iOS 16–26 can be jailbroken. Confirm that your exact device and firmware already have a compatible rootless jailbreak before purchasing. The official listing does not claim RootHide support, so RootHide users should not infer compatibility from the word “rootless.”

Who benefits most from 1.1.0?

The Dual-SIM change is the clearest upgrade for people who actively use two lines, because both strengths can be seen without returning to the stock indicator layout. The network label also makes the capsule more informative when Wi-Fi is unavailable, while the custom time format is useful for anyone who wants the weekday or seconds visible without adding another status-bar tweak.

Notification filtering matters if three icon slots fill with low-priority apps. Limit the feature to messaging, work or security apps whose pending notifications you genuinely want to see at a glance.

Update and test safely

  1. Open the package manager configured for your rootless jailbreak and refresh sources.
  2. Confirm the package identifier is com.karin722.lilywhite and the offered version is 1.1.0 from Havoc.
  3. Install the update and respring when prompted.
  4. Open lilywhite’s preferences and review the new time-format and notification-app settings before changing several options at once.
  5. Test the Home Screen, Lock Screen and an app, then open and close Control Center to confirm the old icon-ghosting behavior is gone.
  6. On a Dual-SIM phone, verify that both rows respond to changes in signal strength. Away from Wi-Fi, check that the expected 5G, 4G or LTE label appears.

Possible conflicts

The developer warns that other tweaks which change the time, Wi-Fi, signal or battery area may conflict. Disable overlapping status-bar modifications before deciding that lilywhite itself is broken. The Dynamic Island spacing fix reduces overlap with an expanding island, but it does not establish compatibility with every separate Dynamic Island tweak.

If a display problem remains, lilywhite includes diagnostic logging. Reproduce the issue and send the log with your iPhone model, iOS version, jailbreak and lilywhite version through the developer’s support links on Havoc. The listing says the log does not include notification text or app names.

Should existing users update?

Yes if you use Dual SIM, want the network type visible, need a custom time format, want notification filtering or have seen stock icons briefly reappear around Control Center. The release notes are specific enough to justify the update, but they do not promise broader jailbreak or device support.
