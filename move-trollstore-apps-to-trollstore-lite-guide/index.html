How to Move TrollStore Apps to TrollStore Lite on a Jailbroken iPhone

TrollStore Lite 2.1.1 can detect apps managed by the full TrollStore and transfer their management to the jailbreak-based edition. Use its built-in transfer command, then resolve app registration or icon issues with the matching Settings utility.

By Zeeshan Barvi · Published October 8, 2026 · Verified against the official 2.1.1 release, source code and Havoc package data

Important: TrollStore Lite is not a jailbreak. Havoc lists package compatibility from iOS 14.0 through 26.0.1 and marks the package rootless, but you still need a functioning compatible jailbreak and its code-signing bypass. That package range does not prove jailbreak availability for every device or firmware.

Full TrollStore and TrollStore Lite are different

The full TrollStore uses a CoreTrust bug to keep supported apps permanently signed on specific firmware: iOS 14 beta 2 through 16.6.1, iOS 16.7 RC (20H18), and iOS 17.0 according to the official 2.1.1 README. TrollStore Lite instead relies on an active jailbreak’s code-signing bypass, so it can work outside that CoreTrust window only where a compatible jailbreak already exists.

The transfer tool does not install a jailbreak or widen either project’s compatibility. In the 2.1.1 source, it processes apps that TrollStore Lite recognizes as inactive full-TrollStore installations, re-signs their bundles for the Lite environment, switches their TrollStore marker, and registers the resulting app paths. Treat the transfer as a change in signing and management—not as a new IPA download.

Before you transfer

  1. Update TrollStore Lite to 2.1.1. The April 1, 2026 GitHub release fixes a Lite entitlement typo that could stop apps working as expected under Dopamine. Havoc updated the package listing on April 2.
  2. Use the official Havoc package. Havoc identifies it as com.opa334.trollstorelite, rootless, with ldid as the package dependency in the tagged control file.
  3. Confirm the source apps are in full TrollStore. The transfer row appears only when the app detects inactive TrollStore-managed app bundles. No detected apps means no transfer row.
  4. Close the apps being transferred. Keep a normal backup of important app data. The source implements an in-place bundle operation, but no migration should be your only backup.

Move the apps to TrollStore Lite

  1. Install or update TrollStore Lite 2.1.1 from the official Havoc listing while your compatible rootless jailbreak is active.
  2. Open TrollStore Lite, then select the Settings tab.
  3. Under Utilities, tap Transfer number TrollStore Apps. The number should match the full-TrollStore apps detected for transfer.
  4. Read the confirmation and tap Continue. Keep the app open while the helper processes the bundles.
  5. Return to the Apps tab and verify each expected app is listed.
  6. If a transferred app does not appear on the Home Screen, return to Settings and tap Respring. Use Rebuild Icon Cache only if the icon remains missing.

What AppSync changes

EnvironmentRegistration modeWhat to expect
AppSync Unified installedUser registrationHavoc says registrations persist automatically. The Settings footer explicitly reports this mode when AppSync is detected.
No AppSync UnifiedSystem registrationApps can still be registered through TrollStore Lite, but the listing says they may need to be refreshed after registration is lost.

If an app remains listed in TrollStore Lite but no longer launches or appears registered, open Settings and choose Refresh App Registrations. That is the official recovery path for registrations; repeatedly reinstalling the IPA is not the first step.

Troubleshooting the transfer

SymptomWhat it meansSafe response
No Transfer rowTrollStore Lite did not detect any inactive full-TrollStore app bundles.Confirm the apps were installed through full TrollStore, that the jailbreak is active, and that Lite is version 2.1.1. Do not invent a transfer by reinstalling random IPAs.
Some apps remain after an errorThe helper supports partial failure and reports how many apps could not be transferred.Use Copy Debug Log in the failure alert and preserve it for the developer. Verify the successfully moved apps before retrying.
App is listed but does not launchThe registration may be stale, or an older Lite build may contain the Dopamine entitlement typo.Update to 2.1.1, then use Refresh App Registrations.
Home Screen icon is missingSpringBoard or the icon cache has not caught up with the new registration.Try Respring first; use Rebuild Icon Cache if needed.
App itself is incompatibleSuccessful signing does not guarantee that an old app supports the current iOS version or hardware.Check the app developer’s requirements. TrollStore Lite cannot repair an incompatible binary.

Which edition should manage your apps?

ChooseWhen it fitsMain limitation
Full TrollStoreYour exact firmware is supported and you want CoreTrust-based permasigning that does not depend on an active jailbreak.Official full-version support stops at the named CoreTrust-compatible firmware, including iOS 17.0 but not later general releases.
TrollStore LiteYour device already has a compatible jailbreak and you want TrollStore’s IPA-management interface using that jailbreak’s signing bypass.Its apps depend on the jailbreak environment and registration method; Lite is not a substitute for jailbreak availability.

Quick checklist

Version: TrollStore Lite 2.1.1.

Environment: active compatible rootless jailbreak.

Command: Settings → Transfer TrollStore Apps.

Missing app: Refresh App Registrations.

Missing icon: Respring, then rebuild icon cache.
