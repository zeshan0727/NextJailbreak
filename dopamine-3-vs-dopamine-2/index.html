Dopamine 3 vs Dopamine 2: Compatibility Ranges and What Changed

Dopamine 3 is described as a rootless, semi-untethered jailbreak, like Dopamine 2. The clearest documented change is the expansion of the listed compatibility ranges. Dopamine 3 supports iOS 15.0 through 17.3.1 on arm64e, iOS 15.0 through 18.7.1 on arm64, and iOS 15.0 through 18.7.1 on A12 and A13 devices. The README also lists iOS 26.0 through 26.0.1 for A12 and A13. The supplied facts do not establish broader feature, performance, safety, or installation differences between the two versions.

A12 and A13 devices also have a separate listed range covering iOS 26.0 through 26.0.1. That entry is limited to those chip families and those exact point releases. It should not be extended to other devices or to later iOS 26 versions.

Rootless and semi-untethered: what the descriptions confirm

The official project descriptions characterize Dopamine as rootless and semi-untethered. Rootless jailbreaks permit writes only to certain filesystem areas rather than providing unrestricted filesystem access.

That limitation can affect tweak compatibility. Tweaks may need rootless-specific updates or recompilation. Sileo and Zebra are identified as package managers associated with Dopamine, but the supplied facts do not define a distinct Dopamine 2 versus Dopamine 3 package-manager feature set.
