How to Uninstall TrollStore Safely—and Choose Whether to Keep Apps

TrollStore’s official removal prompt gives you two materially different choices: delete the apps it installed, or leave them in place. The persistence helper is removed separately.

By Zeeshan Barvi · Published October 21, 2026 · Verified against the official TrollStore 2.1.1 release and tagged source

Quick answer

In the standard TrollStore app, open Settings and select Uninstall TrollStore. The official 2.1.1 source presents two choices:

  • Uninstall TrollStore, Uninstall Apps removes TrollStore and the apps installed through it.
  • Uninstall TrollStore, Preserve Apps removes TrollStore but passes the project’s preserve-apps option so those installed apps are left on the device.

The separate Uninstall Persistence Helper control does not mean the same thing. It restores the helper’s host app to its original state and removes your persistent way to refresh TrollStore app registrations.

Back up first. Export documents, game saves, authentication recovery material, and any other irreplaceable data from TrollStore-installed apps before choosing either removal path. The project’s prompt defines what happens to installed apps; it is not a guarantee about every app’s private data or future launch state.

Choose the outcome before you tap uninstall

Official choiceWhat it doesWhen it makes sense
Uninstall TrollStore, Uninstall AppsRemoves TrollStore-managed apps along with TrollStore.You want a clean removal and have already exported anything important.
Uninstall TrollStore, Preserve AppsRemoves TrollStore while leaving its installed apps registered for now.You need the apps left in place and understand that future repair, update, or removal may require reinstalling TrollStore.
Uninstall Persistence HelperReverts the selected helper host app and removes persistent registration-refresh access.You also want to remove the helper. This is a separate action, not one of the two TrollStore uninstall choices.

How to uninstall TrollStore

  1. Open the standard TrollStore app. This guide is for the normal TrollStore interface, not TrollStore Lite.
  2. Open Settings. Scroll to the action labelled Uninstall TrollStore.
  3. Read the confirmation sheet. Confirm that you see the two official choices described above.
  4. Choose your app outcome. Select Uninstall Apps for a clean removal, or Preserve Apps to leave TrollStore-installed apps on the device.
  5. Let the process finish. Do not interrupt the removal or manually delete TrollStore’s files while it is working.

The names above are not editorial shorthand: they are the button labels in TrollStore 2.1.1’s tagged shared Settings implementation. The same source shows that Preserve Apps adds the preserve-apps argument to the uninstall command.

If you choose Preserve Apps

Preserving apps is useful when you are removing TrollStore temporarily or want to avoid immediately deleting app containers. It does not guarantee that every preserved app will remain launchable forever. TrollStore’s README explains that its persistence helper can reregister apps after an icon-cache reload. Once TrollStore and that helper are gone, you no longer have the normal TrollStore route to refresh registrations.

You also lose TrollStore’s normal management interface. If you later need to update, repair, or reliably remove a preserved app, reinstalling a compatible TrollStore setup may be the cleanest route. Do not assume that swiping away an icon or using an unrelated signing tool is equivalent to TrollStore’s own app removal.

If you choose Uninstall Apps

This is the clearer option when you want TrollStore and its installed apps removed together. Before selecting it, open each app that holds local-only information and export what you need. The official confirmation label establishes that the apps are removed; it does not promise that every developer stores or syncs data in the same way.

If you only want to remove one TrollStore-installed app, do not uninstall TrollStore. The official README says to remove that app from TrollStore’s Apps tab. See the TrollStore IPA guide for the same management boundary.

Remove the persistence helper separately

If you want a full removal, deal with the persistence helper before uninstalling TrollStore so its Settings page is still available:

  1. Open TrollStore and go to Settings.
  2. Select Uninstall Persistence Helper.
  3. Read the warning, then confirm if you no longer need registration refresh.

The official 2.1.1 source treats this as its own command. When run from the helper host app, the warning says that uninstalling the helper reverts that app to its original state but removes the persistent ability to refresh TrollStore’s registrations. Removing the helper alone does not uninstall TrollStore or its installed apps.

If your apps are disappearing or no longer opening and you are not trying to remove TrollStore, stop here and use the persistence-helper repair guide instead.

TrollStore Lite is different

The standard Settings source conditionally excludes the Uninstall TrollStore row from TrollStore Lite builds. That means this exact menu path should not be projected onto Lite. If you use TrollStore Lite inside a jailbreak environment, follow that environment’s current removal method rather than forcing the standard TrollStore steps.

Removing standard TrollStore is also not the same as removing Dopamine, RootHide, bootstrap files, or another jailbreak. These are separate products and persistence layers.

Compatibility boundary

TrollStore 2.1.1 was released on April 1, 2026 and remained the latest official release when this guide was checked. Its official README lists support for iOS and iPadOS 14.0 beta 2 through 16.6.1, iOS 16.7 RC build 20H18, and iOS 17.0. It explicitly says other iOS 16.7.x versions and iOS 17.0.1 or newer are unsupported without another suitable CoreTrust bug.

That compatibility list describes where TrollStore can operate. It does not prove that every device on a different version can install it, nor does it turn the uninstall flow into a jailbreak remover. If you are evaluating installation methods, use the TrollStore installation method guide.

Removal checklist

  • Exported irreplaceable data from every TrollStore-installed app.
  • Decided whether installed apps should be removed or preserved.
  • Removed the persistence helper first if a full cleanup is the goal.
  • Used TrollStore’s own Settings action instead of deleting internal files.
  • Did not confuse TrollStore removal with jailbreak removal.

Frequently asked questions

Does Preserve Apps keep the apps working forever?

No guarantee. The option leaves them installed during TrollStore removal, but removing TrollStore and its helper also removes the standard registration-refresh and management path.

Does removing the persistence helper remove TrollStore?

No. TrollStore 2.1.1 exposes separate actions for the helper and TrollStore itself.

Can I remove one TrollStore app from the Home Screen?

The official README directs users to uninstall TrollStore-installed apps from TrollStore’s Apps tab. Use that route while TrollStore is present.

Will uninstalling TrollStore remove my jailbreak?

No. TrollStore removal and jailbreak removal are separate procedures.
