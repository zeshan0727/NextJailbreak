Rocket for Instagram 4.18.0 adds sticker copying, Stories tray controls and link-sharing privacy

Rocket for Instagram version 4.18.0 is listed with three headline changes: copying stickers from comments on posts and Reels, hiding friend suggestions from the Stories tray, and disabling tracking parameters on supported links that users copy or share. The project describes Rocket as an all-in-one Instagram tweak with media, privacy and interface controls. Its package information lists support for iOS 15.0 or later, requires the Instagram app from the App Store, and records builds for iphoneos-arm, iphoneos-arm64 and iphoneos-arm64e architectures.

Key points

  • Rocket for Instagram 4.18.0 is the version listed by the project’s package page and changelog.
  • The release adds sticker copying from comments on posts and Reels.
  • A new option can hide friend suggestions from the Stories tray.
  • “Disable link tracking” removes tracking parameters from supported links copied or shared through Instagram.
  • The project states compatibility with devices running iOS 15.0 or later.
  • The App Store Instagram app is required, according to the project page.

Rocket 4.18.0 focuses on sharing and presentation

Rocket for Instagram is presented by its project as an all-in-one tweak for Instagram. Version 4.18.0 is a relatively focused release, with its changelog calling out three additions rather than a broad redesign of the application. The update covers copying stickers from comments, controlling friend suggestions in the Stories tray, and removing tracking parameters from supported links.

These changes fit into existing Rocket categories: media reuse, control over Instagram’s presentation, and privacy-oriented sharing options. The source does not claim that every sticker, link or Instagram surface is covered. In particular, the changelog identifies comments on posts and Reels for sticker copying and qualifies the link feature by referring to supported links.

  • Copy stickers from comments on posts and Reels.
  • Hide friend suggestions from the Stories tray.
  • Remove tracking parameters from supported links that are copied or shared.

Sticker copying applies to comments on posts and Reels

The first 4.18.0 addition is support for copying stickers from comments on posts and Reels. This gives users a way to reuse that specific type of comment content without relying only on Rocket’s broader download and reposting tools.

The available release information does not explain the exact gesture, the destination format, or whether copied stickers retain associated metadata. It also does not extend the claim to stickers found throughout Instagram. The safest reading is the narrow one stated in the changelog: the feature applies to stickers in comments on posts and Reels.

Rocket already lists several media-related capabilities, including downloading Feed media, Reels, profile videos, Stories, Instants and supported visual messages in direct messages. It also lists reposting Feed posts and Reels, copying captions and comments, copying media links, and opening media full-screen. Sticker copying adds another specific content-handling option, but the source does not describe it as a replacement for those existing tools.

A new option can hide friend suggestions from the Stories tray

Rocket 4.18.0 also adds an option to hide friend suggestions from the Stories tray. The change is aimed at the area used to access Stories and gives users a way to remove that category of suggestion from the tray’s presentation.

The changelog does not explain how Instagram selects friend suggestions, whether hiding them affects recommendations elsewhere in the app, or whether the option applies globally or only to a particular account. Rocket separately advertises settings profiles that can be mapped to specific Instagram accounts, but the 4.18.0 release notes do not explicitly say that the new Stories tray setting is included in those profiles.

That distinction matters for expectations. The documented feature is a control for friend suggestions in the Stories tray. The source does not say that it changes Instagram’s recommendation system across the entire application or alters which accounts Instagram recommends in other locations.

Link tracking can be disabled for supported links

The third highlighted change is a setting named “Disable link tracking.” According to the changelog, it removes tracking parameters from supported links that users copy or share. The option is therefore intended to provide more control over the information carried by certain links when they leave Instagram.

The wording “supported links” places an important limit on the feature. The project does not promise removal of every tracking parameter from every URL, and it does not define all supported domains, parameters or sharing paths. It also does not claim to block tracking performed by a destination website after a link has opened.

Users who rely on referral, campaign or attribution information should review the resulting link before sharing it. Removing parameters can change how a recipient’s visit is attributed. Conversely, users seeking a cleaner shared link should treat the setting as a Rocket feature with the coverage described by the project, not as a universal link-privacy system.

  • The setting concerns supported links copied or shared through supported paths.
  • The source does not define universal URL or domain coverage.
  • The feature is not described as blocking tracking performed by a destination website.

Rocket’s wider feature set

Beyond the new additions, the project lists a broad collection of media, privacy and interface controls. Media features include downloads for several Instagram surfaces, reposting Feed posts and Reels, copying captions and comments, copying media links, and a downloads manager with previews, filters, sharing, retries and bulk deletion.

Privacy-related controls cover selected Story and direct-message behaviors. Rocket lists private browsing controls for seen status, playback, typing and screenshot behavior, along with Story viewer-list search. It also offers Discreet mode, which hides Rocket buttons from screenshots, and options related to locally marking media as seen. These descriptions concern the controls named by the project and should not be expanded into claims about complete anonymity or universal privacy protection.

The interface list includes List and Grid home-feed layouts, Reels Picture in Picture, controls for infinite Reels scrolling, clickable web addresses, themes, custom fonts, custom icons and tab-bar customization. The project also lists settings profiles that can be mapped to Instagram accounts, customization of feed layouts and dates, follow-back status, enlarged profile pictures and locally customized profile statistics. Call recording is listed for Instagram voice calls and for audio and video calls with participant video and call audio.

Earlier changelog entries show that Rocket has continued adding compatibility and customization work. Previous releases mention higher-quality downloads, additional languages, Safari link handling, custom fonts, custom icons, app-wide themes, grid-feed controls and compatibility references for particular Instagram releases. Those historical notes show the feature set has changed over time, but they do not establish a specific Instagram version requirement for 4.18.0.

  • Media tools include downloads, reposting, copying captions and comments, and copying media links.
  • Privacy controls cover selected Story and direct-message behaviors.
  • Interface tools include layouts, themes, fonts, icons and tab-bar customization.
  • Settings profiles can be mapped to specific Instagram accounts, according to the project page.

Compatibility and installation information

The project page states that Rocket is compatible with all devices running iOS 15.0 or later. Package records for version 4.18.0 list firmware 15.0 or later and provide entries for iphoneos-arm, iphoneos-arm64 and iphoneos-arm64e architectures. The package metadata also lists mobilesubstrate as a dependency.

Rocket requires the Instagram app from the App Store, according to the project page. The same page presents options to add the source to Sileo, Zebra or Cydia, as well as an option to sideload without a jailbreak. Because several installation routes are presented, the appropriate method depends on the user’s device and software setup. The supplied release material does not provide one universal installation procedure for every environment.

The source does not state a specific Instagram build requirement for version 4.18.0. Users should therefore distinguish between the documented iOS requirement and Instagram compatibility. Before installing or updating, they can compare their iOS version with the project’s stated range, confirm that the App Store Instagram app is installed, and check current compatibility information for their particular Instagram release.

  • The stated operating-system range is iOS 15.0 or later.
  • Version 4.18.0 package records list arm, arm64 and arm64e builds.
  • The package metadata lists firmware 15.0 or later and mobilesubstrate.
  • The App Store Instagram app is required.
  • The source does not give a specific Instagram version requirement for 4.18.0.

What users should expect from the update

Rocket 4.18.0 is most relevant to users who want more control over everyday Instagram sharing and presentation. Sticker copying addresses a specific reuse case in comments on posts and Reels. The Stories tray option removes friend suggestions from that location. The link setting offers a choice to remove tracking parameters from supported links before they are copied or shared.

None of these release notes describes a new feed system, universal URL cleaning or a complete privacy layer for Instagram. The practical question is whether one of the three documented additions matches a user’s workflow. Existing Rocket features remain extensive, but their behavior can depend on the Instagram environment in which the tweak is used, and the source does not guarantee that every listed feature works with every Instagram release.
